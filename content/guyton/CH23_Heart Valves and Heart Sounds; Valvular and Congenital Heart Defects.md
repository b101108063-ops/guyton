## 章節導讀

心臟瓣膜的功能是確保血液在心臟與血管之間沿正確方向流動。本章首先說明<span style="color:#22d3ee">心臟</span>四個瓣膜——<span style="color:#22d3ee">mitral valve</span>、<span style="color:#22d3ee">tricuspid valve</span>、<span style="color:#22d3ee">aortic valve</span> 與 <span style="color:#22d3ee">pulmonary valve</span>——的解剖構造與生理功能，以及<span style="color:#60a5fa">心音</span>（<span style="color:#60a5fa">heart sounds</span>）是如何在<span style="color:#22d3ee">心室</span>收縮與瓣膜關閉時產生的。第二部分聚焦於<span style="color:#60a5fa">瓣膜疾病</span>（<span style="color:#60a5fa">valvular heart disease</span>），說明 <span style="color:#60a5fa">stenosis</span>（狹窄）與 <span style="color:#60a5fa">regurgitation</span>（逆流）兩種主要病變如何改變<span style="color:#22d3ee">心臟</span>內的血流動力學，並導致特定的<span style="color:#60a5fa">心雜音</span>（<span style="color:#60a5fa">murmurs</span>）。第三部分介紹常見的<span style="color:#60a5fa">先天性心臟缺損</span>（<span style="color:#60a5fa">congenital heart defects</span>），包括<span style="color:#60a5fa">心房中隔缺損</span>、<span style="color:#60a5fa">心室中隔缺損</span>與 <span style="color:#22d3ee">patent ductus arteriosus</span>，以及它們如何造成血液<span style="color:#60a5fa">分流</span>（<span style="color:#60a5fa">shunting</span>）與特定的臨床表徵。理解這些病理變化，對於掌握<span style="color:#60a5fa">心衰竭</span>、<span style="color:#60a5fa">呼吸困難</span>與<span style="color:#60a5fa">發紺</span>（<span style="color:#60a5fa">cyanosis</span>）的根源至關重要。

## 心臟瓣膜的結構與功能

心臟有四組瓣膜，分為兩大類：

### <span style="color:#60a5fa">Atrioventricular Valves</span>（房室瓣）

- **<span style="color:#22d3ee">Mitral valve</span>（左房室瓣）**：位於<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心房</span>與<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心室</span>之間，有兩個尖瓣（<span style="color:#22d3ee">cusp</span>），由<span style="color:#22d3ee">chordae tendineae</span>（腱索）與<span style="color:#22d3ee">乳頭肌</span>（<span style="color:#22d3ee">papillary muscles</span>）固定，防止收縮期血液逆流回<span style="color:#22d3ee">心房</span>
- **<span style="color:#22d3ee">Tricuspid valve</span>（右房室瓣）**：位於<span style="color:#22d3ee">右心房</span>與<span style="color:#22d3ee">右心室</span>之間，有三個尖瓣，功能同上

### <span style="color:#60a5fa">Semilunar Valves</span>（半月瓣）

- **<span style="color:#22d3ee">Aortic valve</span>（主動脈瓣）**：位於<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心室</span>與<span style="color:#22d3ee">主動脈</span>之間，三個半月瓣，<span style="color:#22d3ee">主動脈竇</span>（<span style="color:#22d3ee">sinus of Valsalva</span>）的冠狀竇協助在心舒張期填充<span style="color:#22d3ee">冠狀动脉</span>
- **<span style="color:#22d3ee">Pulmonary valve</span>（肺动脉瓣）**：位於<span style="color:#22d3ee">右心室</span>與<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺动脉</span>之間，三個半月瓣，功能為防止<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺动脉</span>內血液逆流回<span style="color:#22d3ee">右心室</span>

### 瓣膜如何防止逆流

房室瓣的關閉依賴兩個機制：心室內壓上升的力量推動瓣膜關閉，而乳頭肌的適時收縮透過腱索拉住瓣膜，防止瓣膜翻轉進入心房。半月瓣的關閉則完全依賴心室舒張期主動脈/肺动脉內較高的壓力，將瓣膜推向關閉位置。

## 心音（Heart Sounds）

### <span style="color:#60a5fa">第一心音</span>（S1）

<span style="color:#60a5fa">S1</span> 產生於<span style="color:#22d3ee">心室</span>收縮開始時，代表 <span style="color:#22d3ee">mitral valve</span> 與 <span style="color:#22d3ee">tricuspid valve</span> 的關閉。聲音的強度取決於：

- <span style="color:#22d3ee">心室</span>收縮的速率（速率越快，聲音越響）
- 瓣膜葉片關閉的完整性

### <span style="color:#60a5fa">第二心音</span>（S2）

<span style="color:#60a5fa">S2</span> 產生於<span style="color:#22d3ee">心室</span>舒張開始時，代表 <span style="color:#22d3ee">aortic valve</span> 與 <span style="color:#22d3ee">pulmonary valve</span> 的關閉。Aortic valve 的關閉通常領先於 pulmonary valve，兩者之間的間隔稱為 A2-P2 interval。正常吸氣時，由於胸腔內壓改變，P2 延遲，A2-P2 interval 加寬（生理性分裂）；某些病理狀況下，這種分裂會更加明顯或固定不變。

### 第三心音（S3）與第四心音（S4）

- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">S3</span>（<span style="color:#60a5fa">Third heart sound</span>）**：出現在 <span style="color:#60a5fa">S2</span> 後的 <span style="color:#60a5fa">early diastole</span>，與 <span style="color:#60a5fa">rapid ventricular filling</span> 期血液快速進入<span style="color:#22d3ee">心室</span>有關，正常可在年輕人聽到；若出現在中年人或老年人，則常表示<span style="color:#22d3ee">心室</span><span style="color:#60a5fa">舒張功能障礙</span>或容積過負荷
- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">S4</span>（<span style="color:#60a5fa">Fourth heart sound</span>）**：出現在 <span style="color:#60a5fa">S1</span> 之前的 <span style="color:#60a5fa">late diastole</span>，與<span style="color:#22d3ee">心房</span>收縮將血液主動推入<span style="color:#22d3ee">心室</span>有關；常見於<span style="color:#22d3ee">心肌</span>順應度下降的情況（如 <span style="color:#60a5fa">hypertensive heart disease</span>、<span style="color:#60a5fa">aortic stenosis</span>）

## 瓣膜疾病

瓣膜疾病可分為 stenosis（狹窄）與 regurgitation（逆流）兩種基本型態，有時兩者會同時存在。

### <span style="color:#60a5fa">Aortic Stenosis</span>（主動脈瓣狹窄）

主動脈瓣葉因<span style="color:#c084fc">風濕性心臟病</span>而黏連融合，導致瓣膜開放受限。生理學後果包括：

- **<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心室</span><span style="color:#60a5fa">後負荷</span>（<span style="color:#60a5fa">afterload</span>）增加**：<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心室</span>必須用更大的力量才能將血液泵過狹窄的瓣口
- **<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心室</span><span style="color:#60a5fa">肥厚</span>（<span style="color:#60a5fa">hypertrophy</span>）**：為代償增加的<span style="color:#60a5fa">後負荷</span>，<span style="color:#22d3ee">心肌</span>細胞增生，但肥厚使順應度下降，<span style="color:#60a5fa">舒張功能障礙</span>
- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">心輸出量</span>維持但運動耐量下降**：即使 <span style="color:#60a5fa">LVH</span>，隨運動需求增加，<span style="color:#60a5fa">心輸出量</span>仍無法相應提升
- **狹窄後<span style="color:#f87171">低血壓</span>與暈厥**：運動時<span style="color:#60a5fa">血管舒張</span>但<span style="color:#60a5fa">心輸出量</span>無法增加，導致 <span style="color:#f87171">systemic hypotension</span>

典型主動狹窄雜音：粗糙的 systolic crescendo-decrescendo murmur，於主動區（right second intercostal space）最響，發射至頸部。

### <span style="color:#60a5fa">Mitral Stenosis</span>（二尖瓣狹窄）

二尖瓣葉黏連融合，導致舒張期血流由<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心房</span>進入<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心室</span>受阻。生理學後果：

- **<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心房</span>壓力升高與擴大**：為克服狹窄瓣膜的阻力，<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心房</span>內壓必須高於<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心室</span>
- **<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺靜脈</span><span style="color:#f87171">高壓</span>**：傳導至肺部，導致 <span style="color:#22d3ee">pulmonary congestion</span> 與<span style="color:#60a5fa">呼吸困難</span>
- **<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺高壓</span>**：慢性<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺靜脈</span><span style="color:#f87171">高壓</span>最終引起<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺血管</span>反應性收縮，導致 <span style="color:#22d3ee">pulmonary hypertension</span>
- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">心輸出量</span>下降**：LV filling 受損，導致 forward output 減少

典型二尖瓣狹窄雜音：low-pitched diastolic rumble，於心尖區（apex）最響，常伴有 opening snap（開放音）。

### <span style="color:#60a5fa">Mitral Regurgitation</span>（二尖瓣逆流）

二尖瓣關閉不全，導致收縮期血液逆流回<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心房</span>。生理學後果：

- **<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心房</span>與<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心室</span><span style="color:#60a5fa">容量負荷過重</span>**：<span style="color:#60a5fa">每搏輸出量</span>的一部分逆流回<span style="color:#22d3ee">心房</span>，使<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心房</span>與<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心室</span>皆擴大
- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Systolic murmur</span>**：全收縮期吹風樣 murmur，於心尖區最響
- **<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺靜脈</span><span style="color:#f87171">高壓</span>與<span style="color:#f87171">肺水腫</span>**：長期<span style="color:#60a5fa">容量負荷</span>導致<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心房</span>擴大與<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺靜脈</span>逆流壓力升高

### <span style="color:#60a5fa">Aortic Regurgitation</span>（主動脈瓣逆流）

舒張期血液從<span style="color:#22d3ee">主動脈</span>逆流回<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心室</span>。生理學後果：

- **<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心室</span><span style="color:#60a5fa">容量負荷過重</span>**：每次收縮時，除了正常排出的血液外，還要容納逆流的血液，導致<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心室</span>擴大
- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Wide pulse pressure</span>（寬脈壓）**：收縮壓升高、舒張壓降低（因為血液逆流），脈壓明顯增大
- **<span style="color:#60a5fa">Diastolic decrescendo murmur</span>**：舒張早期 high-pitched decrescendo murmur，於<span style="color:#22d3ee">胸骨</span>左緣最響

## 先天性心臟缺損（Congenital Heart Defects）

<span style="color:#60a5fa">先天性心臟缺損</span>是最常見的出生缺陷之一，約占所有出生的 <span style="color:#34d399">1%</span>。主要可分為<span style="color:#60a5fa">非發紺性</span>（<span style="color:#60a5fa">acyanotic</span>）與<span style="color:#60a5fa">發紺性</span>（<span style="color:#60a5fa">cyanotic</span>）缺損兩類。

### 心室中隔缺損（Ventricular Septal Defect, VSD）

最常見的先天性心臟病。心室中隔上有孔洞，導致收縮期血液由左心室分流至右心室。缺損大小決定了分流量：

- **<span style="color:#f87171">小 VSD</span>**：<span style="color:#60a5fa">分流量</span>少，症狀輕微，可能自行關閉；產生粗糙的全收縮期 murmur
- **<span style="color:#f87171">大 VSD</span>**：大量左至右<span style="color:#60a5fa">分流</span> → <span style="color:#22d3ee">右心室</span>與<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺循環</span>過負荷 → <span style="color:#22d3ee">pulmonary hypertension</span> → eventually <span style="color:#60a5fa">Eisenmenger syndrome</span>（當<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺血管</span>阻力超過全身阻力時，<span style="color:#60a5fa">分流</span>逆轉為右至左，病人出現<span style="color:#60a5fa">發紺</span>）

### 心房中隔缺損（Atrial Septal Defect, ASD）

<span style="color:#22d3ee">心房</span>中隔上有孔洞，血液由<span style="color:#22d3ee">左心房</span><span style="color:#60a5fa">分流</span>至<span style="color:#22d3ee">右心房</span>，通常在兒童期症狀輕微。大量<span style="color:#60a5fa">分流</span>可導致<span style="color:#22d3ee">右心室</span>與<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺循環</span>過負荷。聽診特徵包括：fixed split <span style="color:#60a5fa">S2</span>（因吸氣對<span style="color:#22d3ee">右心房</span>血流影響被緩衝）與 systolic ejection murmur（於 <span style="color:#22d3ee">pulmonary area</span>）。

### Patent Ductus Arteriosus（PDA，開放性動脈導管）

胎兒時期連接<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺动脉</span>與<span style="color:#22d3ee">主動脈</span>的 <span style="color:#22d3ee">ductus arteriosus</span> 出生後未閉合。在新生兒，給予 <span style="color:#fb923c">indomethacin</span>（<span style="color:#fb923c">prostaglandin synthesis inhibitor</span>）可促進關閉；若持續開放，血液由高壓的<span style="color:#22d3ee">主動脈</span>持續流向低壓的<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺动脉</span>，造成<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺循環</span>過負荷。典型雜音為機器樣連續性 murmur（machinery murmur），於左鎖骨下區最響。

### Eisenmenger Syndrome（艾森曼格症候群）

慢性大量左至右<span style="color:#60a5fa">分流</span>導致<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺血管</span>床發生結構性病變（<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺血管</span>內膜增生與硬化），使<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺血管</span>阻力顯著升高。當<span style="color:#22d3ee">肺血管</span>阻力超過全身血管阻力時，原本左至右的<span style="color:#60a5fa">分流</span>方向逆轉為右至左，靜脈血直接進入體循環，導致<span style="color:#60a5fa">發紺</span>（<span style="color:#60a5fa">cyanosis</span>）與<span style="color:#60a5fa">紅血球增多症</span>（<span style="color:#60a5fa">polycythemia</span>）。

## 瓣膜疾病與心衰竭的整合理解

瓣膜疾病之所以導致心衰竭，是因為它們改變了心臟的壓力或容量負荷，長期下來造成心室結構與功能的進行性病變（remodeling）。理解每種病變的 hemodynamic consequence，是預測其自然病程與選擇適當治療策略（藥物治療、瓣膜修補或置換手術）的基礎。
